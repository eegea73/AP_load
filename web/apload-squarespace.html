For research use only. This calculator normalises prescribed antipsychotic dose for research analyses; it is not a prescribing or clinical decision tool.

Antipsychotic Dose Load

AP_Load2026 calculatorreference table 2026

Each prescribed daily dose as a percentage of a documented reference dose, summed across agents without an upper cap. Clozapine is referenced to a target individualised for sex and cigarettes per day; the other antipsychotics to a fixed dose reconciling the WHO DDD and the licensed maximum.

Sex

Tobacco only. Induction of CYP1A2 saturates at about ten cigarettes per day. E-cigarettes, nicotine replacement and heated-tobacco products count as zero.

Clozapine anchors

The standard configuration tracked measured plasma clozapine better; the alternative is provided for reproducing published comparisons, not as an equivalent option.

Regimen

Add as many antipsychotics as the regimen has; the load is their sum, uncapped. Doses are oral mg/day, or mg per injection interval for depots. Intravenous and short-acting intramuscular doses are not covered.

Reference doses

The dose that scores 100. Oral agents are reconciled as the geometric mean of the licensed maximum and the WHO DDD, capped at 1.5 × DDD; injectables are referenced to the licensed maximum for the same dosing interval; clozapine to an individualised target, shown here for the sex and consumption set above.
